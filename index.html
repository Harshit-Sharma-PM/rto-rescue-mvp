Opportunity map

Four intervention moments

01 · Order created — COD confirmation
Confirm genuine intent. Cancel pre-dispatch only on explicit customer choice or merchant policy.

02 · Before dispatch — address verification
Collect missing flat, landmark or pin details; hold only when required data is invalid.

03 · Before dispatch — prepaid nudge
Offer a voluntary, transparent benefit; preserve COD choice under merchant policy.

04 · NDR raised — WhatsApp + targeted voice
Focused MVP: one carrier + top two NDR reasons. Capture availability/address, submit supported action, track acknowledgement and final delivery. Voice only for eligible high-value COD non-responders.

Delivery recovery workspace

Prevent the return before it happens.

Reduce RTO across all shipped orders: prevent avoidable COD dispatches, improve address quality for COD and prepaid, and rescue delivery exceptions with WhatsApp and targeted voice.

Why RTO matters: Every returned shipment can create avoidable forward/reverse shipping and handling costs, delay inventory and cash recovery, and damage customer experience. The goal is fewer preventable returns—not simply more messages.

Problem & root-cause hypotheses

Two intervention moments across the order journey

Validate in Month 1

Before dispatch
Low-intent COD orders, incomplete addresses or contact details, and customer uncertainty can lead to avoidable shipments.

After dispatch / NDR
Customer unavailable, address problems, rescheduling needs or refusal may cause a failed delivery.

Hypothesis
Resolve intent and address uncertainty before dispatch; use WhatsApp to recover eligible delivery exceptions, with targeted voice only when incremental value can justify the cost.

These are hypotheses, not confirmed market shares. Validate with matured merchant orders, COD/prepaid segments and carrier NDR reason codes.

North Star metric

Business outcome, not message volume

RTO per 100 orders placed

RTO shipments attributable to an order cohort ÷ all eligible orders placed in that cohort × 100

Compare treatment vs control on an intention-to-treat basis. Keep every eligible order placed in the denominator. How voice adds value

Six-month mandate · outcome-led roadmap

Discover, prove, then scale.

The roadmap is organised around reducing uncertainty and proving measurable RTO impact—not just shipping one feature each month. Discovery, feasibility and customer learning continue throughout delivery.

Roadmap principle: Define the baseline and decision criteria first; build the smallest feasible MVP; pilot against a control; scale only when incremental outcomes, unit economics and customer guardrails support it. Timings are planning assumptions and may change with evidence.
MONTH 01 · DISCOVER

Validate the problem

Interview Shopify merchants; analyse mature RTO cohorts by COD/prepaid, carrier and NDR reason; baseline costs and customer pain; define North Star, event tracking and experiment design.

Gate

MONTH 02 · VALIDATE

Feasibility & economics

Check Shopify events, WhatsApp templates, carrier API coverage, consent and operational constraints. Estimate cost per eligible order and incremental contribution. Prototype and usability-test COD Confirm and NDR Rescue.

Gate

MONTH 03 · BUILD

COD Confirm MVP

Build explicit confirm, edit-details and cancellation-request flows; eligibility rules, merchant status visibility, audit events, error handling and QA. Continue NDR/carrier discovery in parallel.

Gate

MONTH 04 · PILOT

Test COD + focused NDR

Run a limited COD pilot with control; monitor outcomes and guardrails. Build/pilot NDR Rescue for one supported carrier and the top one or two actionable NDR reasons; verify carrier acknowledgements and delivery events.

Gate
Is there credible lift without unacceptable harm?
MONTH 05 · OPTIMISE

Improve & test voice

Analyse pilot by segment; tune timing, eligibility and flows. Test targeted voice for eligible unresolved cases against WhatsApp-only; recalculate incremental recovery and channel cost.

Gate

MONTH 06 · SCALE DECISION

Scale, iterate or stop

Evaluate mature cohorts against North Star, control and guardrails. Gradually expand proven interventions; iterate or stop weak performers; document learnings and next priorities.

Why this sequence?

Balance score, order-journey coverage and learning speed.

1 · COD Confirm — prevent
Start before dispatch: explicit intent is actionable early, the workflow is comparatively simple, and it tests whether avoidable COD shipments can be prevented.

2 · NDR Rescue — recover
Second selected initiative, with a 0.80 planning score under the lean-pilot assumptions (effort 1, cost 1). It complements prevention: COD confirmation cannot fix an unavailable customer, an incomplete delivery address or a reschedule need after a failed attempt. Limit scope to one carrier and the top two actionable reasons to keep cost and effort low and learn quickly.

Parallel discovery — address quality and reminders
Their score is higher because assumed reach is broader. Validate the address-error mix, available integrations and incremental lift; bring the best-supported low-risk lever into the pilot rather than pretending the score is irrelevant.

Voice — controlled escalation, not default
Test only for eligible, consented high-value COD non-responders near the reattempt cutoff. Compare incremental recovery with added call cost and customer guardrails.

Decision principle
Use the scorecard to make trade-offs visible. The selected two initiatives cover both prevention and recovery; month-one evidence can change the order if real reach, impact, feasibility or economics differ from these assumptions.
