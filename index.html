Measurement contract

Metrics the product should enforce

North Star

Eligible shipped-order RTO rate on mature outcomes.

Leading indicators

Time to next action, response, carrier acceptance, outcome linkage.

Guardrails

Complaints, opt-outs, invalid updates, duplicate actions and escalations.

Six-month mandate

Prove it before scaling it.

Start with one carrier and the NDR moment; add intelligence and only test voice if it demonstrates incremental lift.

MONTH 01

Discover & baseline

Merchant interviews, RTO reason/cost analysis, validate carrier event and action coverage.

MONTH 02

NDR Rescue MVP

One provider, order matching, reason-specific WhatsApp conversation.

MONTH 03

Execute carrier actions

Supported reattempt/address updates, acknowledgement, retries and human fallback.

MONTH 04

Outcome Intelligence

Link interventions to mature outcomes and run a controlled pilot.

MONTH 05

Test voice selectively

Test eligible unresolved cases only if WhatsApp-first leaves a clear gap.

MONTH 06

Scale what works

Expand carrier coverage only after reliable actions, credible lift and positive economics.

3. Incremental delivery lift
Treatment outperforms control with guardrails intact.

Verified benefit exceeds messaging and operational costs.
